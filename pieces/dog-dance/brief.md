# Dog Disco — brief

**Ask:** "a 2 second dog dancing". **Style:** pixel (135x240, 8 px pixels, PAL16 enforced). **Format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, 24 fps, 2.0s = 48 frames = one bar at 120 BPM, a seamless loop. Music only (composed chiptune), no voice, no assets.

**Story format:** a single-shot loop: a fixed hero on one stage, no cuts or morphs; the last frame flows into frame 0. (No FORMATS.md card covers 2s; this borrows F5's fixed hero and F6's "end = start". Unproven.)

**Idea:** an orange pixel shiba dances one bar on a light-up disco floor. **Device:** one more floor tile lights on each beat; the whole floor flashes on the pose.

**Colour:** orange is the dog's alone (body, ears, arms, tail). Tiles, notes, sign, beams use pink / sky / lime / yellow / purple / ice / white.

**Background life:** disco ball facets turning + sparkles on 8ths; reflections sweeping the wall; spotlight beams swinging on beats; speaker woofers pulsing on beats; notes rising from the speakers; sign bulbs chasing. World time stops in the freeze and runs 1.75s per loop, so every world motion repeats on the loop point.
